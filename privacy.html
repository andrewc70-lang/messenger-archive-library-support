Privacy Policy

Messenger Archive Library
Effective date: 29 October 2026

Publisher check before release: This policy is a draft based on the intended design of an offline archive viewer. Confirm that the released app does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising, network services, or third-party SDKs that collect data. Update this policy and App Store privacy disclosures if its behaviour differs.

Summary

Messenger Archive Library is designed to help you browse supported XML chat archive files on your Mac. Your chat archives may contain personal information, including names, timestamps and message text. Treat them as private documents.

Files and conversations

The app's intended purpose is to read archive files that you choose to open and display their contents to you. The app does not need you to create an account to browse local archives. You should only open files you have the right to access.

Collection and sharing

In the intended offline design, the app does not send the contents of your archives to the developer or a remote service, and does not sell personal information. This statement must be checked against the exact app build before publication. If analytics, diagnostics, cloud features or other network communications are added, this policy must be revised to describe them.

Exports

If you use printing or PDF export, the resulting document may contain the conversation text and other information shown in the app. You choose where to print or save it. Keep exported files secure and share them only with people you trust.

Retention and deletion

Archive files and exported documents are under your control. To remove an archive or exported document, delete it from the location where you saved it using macOS. The app's actual handling of temporary files and preferences should be verified before release.

Children

The app is a general-purpose archive viewer and is not designed to collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app changes. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions or support, please use the project support issue tracker. Do not post chat archives, message contents, passwords, or other private information in a public issue.
